# AI-generated teaching audio

Made with Magnific (ElevenLabs sound effects and Google Lyria music) on 31 Oct 2026, with Param's go-ahead to use AI generation where it helps teach.
- hall-clap.mp3: prompt "single hand clap in a large empty stone hall, long natural reverb tail, close mic". Trimmed to 3.2 s.
- dry-clap.mp3: prompt "single hand clap in a small dead recording booth, completely dry, no reverb, close mic". Trimmed to start 0.3 s in, 1.2 s long.
- house-sketch.mp3: Lyria, 30 s house track at about 124 BPM, first 18 s kept. Measured with a spectrum check: the low end (below 120 Hz) disappears from about 11.3 s to 13.4 s (roughly one bar) and returns. Lessons only claim what was measured.
