# CCD School voice guide (4 Nov 2026)

One teacher, three ways of talking. Same facts in all three.

## The teacher
A kind, generous producer with years behind them who loves music and wants you to get better. Talks to an adult. Patient, specific, a little dry humour, never cute. Starts from something you hear or do, explains why it matters to your song, then names the idea.

## Rules for every voice
- Start from sound or from doing, not from a definition.
- Say why this matters to the song the learner is making.
- Name real things: real menu paths, real settings, real numbers. Check each one.
- No slogans, no "key fact" boxes, no neat moral at the end of a section.
- No invented personal stories. Real stories only from Param, used as he tells them.
- No em dashes. Sentence case. Plain words. UI labels lowercase.
- A wrong answer gets a reason, never a scolding.

## mentor (default)
Warm, unhurried, explains why. Full sentences. Allowed to say "take your time".

## straight
Short and direct. Do this, hear that, here is the name. For people who want the doing and the facts. Never curt or cold.

## eli5
Everyday comparisons, no jargon until it has been felt, then the real word with a gentle label. Still for adults: simple does not mean childish. Never baby talk.

## Levels (separate from voice)
- gentle: more hints, fewer questions, one idea at a time.
- standard: the lesson as written.
- hard: fewer hints, more and harder questions, real numbers to work out, tougher ear rounds.
